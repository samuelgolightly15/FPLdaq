TheFPL Index

Every player in Fantasy Premier League has a price and an owner count. Multiply them and you get the capital tied up in that player. Measured every hour, from the first snapshot on.

The ten largest holdings

Price multiplied by owners, for the ten players carrying the most of it. Rebase to compare movement rather than size.

      Chart two

      The five largest clubs

      Every player at a club, summed. A club rises when managers buy into it, and when the players they already own get more expensive.

          Owners are estimated as the ownership percentage the game publishes, which is rounded to a tenth of a point, applied to the total number of managers. That total climbs steeply before the first deadline, so early rises reflect people registering as much as anything else. Rebasing removes the level but not that effect.
