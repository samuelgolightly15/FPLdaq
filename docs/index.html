      Owners are the ownership percentage the game publishes, rounded to a tenth of a point, applied to the total number of managers. Every player shares that manager count, so it cancels out of a share and these figures are unaffected by the millions of teams registered before the first deadline. Shares always add to 100 per cent, which means a player can drift down while nobody sells them.
